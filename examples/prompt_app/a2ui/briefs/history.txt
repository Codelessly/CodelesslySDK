APP: Skip. A jump rope training app. People use it to plan and time rope sessions, count jumps, and see their progress over weeks.

SCREEN TO BUILD: Training history page: jumps per week as a chart, then the past sessions as a list with date, duration, jumps and best run without a miss.

DATA the screen shows (use exactly these values):
  streak = 4
  weeklyGoal = 5
  sessionsThisWeek = 3
  today = {"name":"Intervals","rounds":8,"work":40,"rest":20,"minutes":8}
  weeks = [{"label":"W39","jumps":2100},{"label":"W40","jumps":2650},{"label":"W41","jumps":2400},{"label":"W42","jumps":3180}]

ACTIONS the screen can trigger: start_session (Begin today's session); open_history (Go to the training history page); open_home (Go to the home screen); open_session (Open one past session (pass its date)); finish_session (End the session (pass the jump count))

THE PERSON IT IS FOR: goal: get fitter, three short sessions a week; experience: started two weeks ago; tone: encouraging and plain, no jargon.
One phone screen, the most important thing first. Make it look like a polished consumer fitness app.
