A music player: the album art (a drawn picture, the set's Media block or shapes of its own; no image URL), the track and artist, a progress line with the elapsed and remaining time, and a transport row with previous, play or pause, and next, the middle one the largest. Parameters: title, artist, art kind, elapsed and length in seconds, playing (a variable the block changes when play or pause is tapped), and events for previous and next.
