        Text.lead "Two years ago every service of ours talked to the others through its own private channel. Last spring we replaced all of them with a single queue. This is what went well, and what we would do differently."
        Heading "Why one queue" level:2 id:why
        Text "Forty services meant forty ways to retry, forty dead-letter conventions and forty dashboards nobody fully trusted. When an incident crossed three services, we spent the first hour just working out where a message had been lost."
        Text "A shared queue gave us one place to look. Ordering, retries and visibility became properties of the platform instead of habits of each team."
        Box pad:[20,4,0,4] border-left:#FF3B6EF5
          Text "The queue did not make our systems simpler. It made their complexity visible, and that was worth more." size:22 weight:600 color:#FF1F2A44
        Heading "What the migration taught us" level:2 id:lessons
        Text "We moved services in waves of five, starting with the ones that could fail without anyone noticing. Each wave used the same small producer wrapper, so every message carried an id, a schema version and a retry budget."
        CodeBlock "producer.ts" "const msg = {\n  id: uuid(),\n  schema: 'orders.v2',\n  retries: 5,\n  body: payload,\n};\nawait queue.publish('orders', msg);" language:"typescript" copyable:true
        Text "The wrapper looks trivial, and that is the point: the less each team had to decide, the faster the waves went."
        Text.strong "Three things we would do again:"
