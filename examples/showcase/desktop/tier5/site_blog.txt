        Text.lead "Two years ago every service on our platform ran its own queue. Last spring we replaced all forty of them with one. Here is what broke, what held, and what we would do again."
        Heading "Why forty queues became a problem" level:2
        Text "Each team had picked the broker that suited them on the day. Over time that gave us six different delivery guarantees, four retry policies and no single place to look when a message went missing. On-call engineers needed a map just to follow one order through the system."
        Text "The cost was not only operational. Every new service started with a week of plumbing, and every incident review ended with the same line: we could not tell where the message was."
