The window is three fixed panes on an app grid of 200 | 320 | fluid | 264: a folder rail on the neutral page tone, a dense white conversation list, then the open thread with its customer panel. Hierarchy comes from type and tint, not boxes. The 22 px Inter Display subject anchors the thread, unread rows go bold with a blue dot, the selected row and folder take the one accent tint, and the internal note (and the composer while writing one) turn amber. Messages, the day rule and the composer share one 720 px reading column, so text never runs too wide.

Everything is driven by state:
- **Folders** filter the list.
- **Search** and the unread-only toggle narrow it further.
- **Clicking a conversation** opens it and marks it read.
- **Reply / Internal note** switches the composer.
- **Send** appends the message, sets the status to Waiting and scrolls to it.
- **Send and close** also sets the status to Closed.

At 768 the customer panel drops away (from 1099 down), and from 899 down the rail collapses to icons, the list narrows to 280 and the composer's tools hide, so nothing overflows.
