A fixed 248 px sidebar and a 72 px top bar frame a main pane that scrolls on its own, as a desktop app does. The content runs top to bottom in order of attention: a four-up figure grid (an auto-fit grid of columns at least 210 wide), then a flexible revenue chart beside a fixed 320 px channel column, then a dense orders table with real column alignment. At 768 the sidebar becomes an icon rail, search hides, the figures wrap to 2×2 and the chart and channel cards stack. The date range swaps the figures, search filters the table, "New order" inserts a draft order, and "View all" expands the table.
