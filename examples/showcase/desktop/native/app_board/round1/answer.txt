The window is a fixed app shell: a 64 px top bar, then a board pane that fills the width beside a 400 px details pane that opens when a card is selected. Every pane scrolls by itself. The lanes are a horizontally scrolling strip of fixed 280 px columns, the way desktop kanban tools work, and each column's cards scroll inside it. At 1280 px all four lanes fit with the details closed. Below 1024 px the members and wordmark drop out of the top bar, and the details pane takes over the whole window while a task is open; close it with × or Escape.

Everything is driven by data:
- **Lanes and cards:** both are repeated from lists. Counts, the filter field and the done meter are computed from the same task list.
- **Moving cards:** you can drag a card into another lane, or change its status in the details pane. Either way it moves and the move is logged in the activity list.
- **Adding tasks:** the lane + buttons and "New task" add a task and open it.
- **Checklist:** ticking an item updates its progress bar.
- **Copy link:** copies the open task's URL to the clipboard.
