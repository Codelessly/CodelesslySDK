Five weakest things:

1. **Broken:** nothing drawn with `customPaint` rendered. The main picture, all four thumbnails and the four related-product cards were empty colour fields, so the gallery had nothing to switch.
2. **Grid:** at 1280 the gallery ended about 150 px above the buying column, leaving a dead hole under the thumbnails. The picture is now taller so the two columns land together.
3. **Thumbnails:** the four thumbnails differed only by background tint. Each now carries a small lamp and its view name, so the switch reads as four views of the product.
4. **Views:** the four views of the main picture were not visibly different. Warm glow, Joint detail and On the desk each now show something of their own: a bloom of light, a ring on the joint, and a desk surface with a plant.
5. **Related products:** the cards had large empty art blocks. Each product is now drawn from path shapes in its own finish, centred on its tinted ground.

The lamp art is now built from `path` nodes and circle boxes inside fixed square stages. The finish choice recolours it through `lampColor`.
