Below it come a white tab card, a four-across related-products grid and a dark footer. At 768 the split, the tab panes and the footer stack vertically, the related grid drops to two columns and the header's nav links hide. The header is sticky.

What works:
- The thumbnails and the arrows (also the ← and → keys) switch the main picture.
- The finish chips recolor the drawn lamp.
- The quantity stepper changes the amount on the "Add to cart" button, and adding a lamp or a related product updates the cart badge.
- "318 reviews" opens the Reviews tab and scrolls to it.
- "Buy now" only adds to the cart and shows a toast, because the store has no checkout page.
