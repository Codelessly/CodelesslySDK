2. **The checkboxes render about 10 px.** In the header and every row they read as faint specks beside 20 px icons and 36 px avatars, and they are poor click targets.
3. **Monthly and Last seen run together.** A right-aligned money column sat 12 px from a left-aligned date column, so "$2,400 2 min ago" read as one phrase. The grid needs a clear gutter there.
4. **The 768 px toolbar wraps badly.** The segment dropdown dropped onto a second line by itself, leaving a ragged two-line toolbar. Search should take the first line, with status and segment sharing the second.
5. **The sidebar nav has no hover feedback.** It also carries a reported `loose_value` on the "Add customer" insert index, which is now fixed.
