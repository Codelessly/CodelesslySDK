MISSING TOKENS
- `radii.surface`, 14: the stat tiles, chart card, channel card and orders card. The theme's `card` radius of 26 is too round for desktop panels.
- `radii.nav`, 9: the sidebar items and the brand mark.
- `radii.headerRow`, 8: the table's column header strip and the "View all" hover area.
- `sizes.sidebar`, 232, and `sizes.sidebarRail`, 72 (below 1024): the app's left pane.
- `sizes.topbar`, 64: the top bar's height. The sidebar brand is centred on its 32 px axis.
- `spacing.gutter`, 24: the content pane's padding and the top bar's side padding.
- `spacing.section`, 16: the gutter of the 4-column page grid, which both the figures and the chart/channel split follow.
- `sizes.tableRow`, 48, and `sizes.tableHead`, 34: the orders table's rows and its header.
- `textStyles.figureLarge`, Inter Display 28 / 800 / -1.0: the stat values. `numberLarge` at 30 is large for four tiles across.
- `textStyles.tableFigure`, Inter Display 14 / 700: table totals and channel amounts.
- `textStyles.tabularNumbers`: no tabular-figures setting exists in the theme.
