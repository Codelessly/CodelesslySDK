Three panes plus a rail, the way a mail client is built: a 216 px folder rail on the gray page, a 336 px conversation list on white, the open conversation filling the middle with its thread and composer capped at 720 so lines stay readable, and a 272 px customer panel back on the gray page with white cards. That gives three tones from left to right, and the eye lands on the white working surface. Each pane scrolls on its own inside an 800 px window. At 768 the customer panel is hidden, the rail shrinks to icons and the list narrows, so the conversation keeps about 400 px.

What works:
- Folders filter the list, and so do search and the "Unread" chip.
- Opening a conversation marks it read and loads its own thread and customer details.
- The status pill cycles Open, Waiting, Closed.
- Reply and Internal note switch the composer, and a note is tinted.
- **Send** adds the message and sets the conversation to Waiting. **Send and close** also closes it.
- ⚡ inserts a canned reply.
