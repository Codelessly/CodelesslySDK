The five weakest things:

1. **Broken:** the document stopped mid-composer, so there was no fence and nothing rendered. Send never existed.
2. **Missing:** the customer side panel (plan, since, last order, three tags) was never written.
3. **Grid:** the pane headers had different heights (list 100, conversation 76). The hairlines under them did not line up across the window.
4. **Proportion:** the four columns left the conversation 456 wide, and its subject, status and assignee were crammed into one row. A long subject wrapped against the pills.
5. **Unfinished:** the composer framed a bordered field inside a bordered box. A filtered list with no matches (Closed plus Unread, or a search) had no empty state.

The fixes:
- The rail is 208, the list 320 and the customer panel 264, so the conversation gets 488.
- Every pane opens with the same 64 px band. The list and the conversation share a second 52 px row (search and properties) and one rule at 116.
- The conversation header is now a subject band above a properties strip holding status, assignee and ticket.
- The composer is a full-width tray that turns amber for internal notes.
- The customer panel is three cards: profile, a 2 × 2 grid of account figures, and tags.
- At 768 the rail becomes icons, the list narrows to 280 and the panel hides.
