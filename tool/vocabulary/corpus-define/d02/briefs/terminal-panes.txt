App: Splitscreen, a terminal multiplexer. A window divided into panes with text output scrolling, split by a key into two side by side or stacked; a focused pane outlined with a title bar; a status line at the bottom with window names as tabs and a clock; a prefix key pressed and a command typed to resize or swap panes; a copy mode with a cursor selecting lines in reverse colour; a session list to switch between; a zoom of one pane to full screen and back.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
