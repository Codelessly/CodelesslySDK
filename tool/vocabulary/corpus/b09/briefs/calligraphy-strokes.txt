App: Brushwork, a calligraphy practice. A large writing square with faint guide lines and a model character shown in order of strokes; the pen path drawn as it is moved, thick and thin by speed; a stroke order animation that plays on tap; a score per stroke for position and direction; a ghost overlay of the model that can be shown or hidden; a practice sheet of repeated squares; paper and ink colour choices; a page of saved work.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
