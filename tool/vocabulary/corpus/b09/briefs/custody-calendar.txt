App: Handoff, a shared custody calendar. A month grid with days coloured by the parent the child is with and a swap day marked with an arrow; a pattern chosen from presets (week on, week off; two two three) that fills the calendar; holiday blocks that override and a school term overlay; a change request sent to the other parent with a reason and an approve or decline; a handover place and time on a day; a child's events listed; an expense split of shared costs; a count of nights each.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
