App: Changeover, a mining shift handover. A cross-section of tunnels as a simple map with levels; faces tagged with notes by the outgoing crew (blast done, water, loose ground) as coloured markers; a list of equipment with fit, faulty or parked states; a photo with scribbled marks; headcount in and out by name; the incoming supervisor swipes to acknowledge each note and then signs; a log of unread items counting down.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
