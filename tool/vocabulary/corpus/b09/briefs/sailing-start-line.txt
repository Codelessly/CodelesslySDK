App: Starting Gun, a sailing race start tool. A countdown from five minutes with a big number and flags shown in order; a sync button that rounds to the nearest minute; a line drawn from two pings at the committee boat and the pin, with a boat dot and the distance to the line in metres updating; a favoured end arrow from wind direction; a bias in degrees; time to burn; a tack timer; a recorded track.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
