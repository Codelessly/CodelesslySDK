App: Drumhead, a seismograph viewer. A tall scrolling paper chart made of rows, each row an hour of wiggly line, a quiet line becoming a burst when a quake arrives; tap a burst to mark the first arrival and the strong part and read magnitude; stations as tabs with the same time lined up; a map with a circle of distance from three stations meeting at an epicentre; a list of events with depth and time.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
