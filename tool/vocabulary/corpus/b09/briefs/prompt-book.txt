App: Cuesheet, a theatre prompt book. A script in two columns with the line spoken and the cue call in the margin; a current-line marker that moves forward with each tap of go; warning, standby and go calls for lights, sound and flys shown as coloured tags that count down; a hold with a reason typed; a rehearsal report with notes by person; a page jump by act and scene; a night-show dark theme that keeps the screen dim.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
