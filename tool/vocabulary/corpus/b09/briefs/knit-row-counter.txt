A big number for the row with plus and minus areas covering the screen; a pattern as a chart of squares in two colours, the current row highlighted, finished rows greyed; repeat counters (decrease every sixth row) that chime; a stitch count with a note; a project list with a progress bar and yarn left in grams; a magnifier strip over the chart; voice command to count; a timer for the session.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls).
