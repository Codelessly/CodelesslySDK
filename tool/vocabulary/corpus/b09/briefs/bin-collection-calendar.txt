App: Binday, a waste collection calendar. A month grid with coloured dots for general, recycling, garden and glass days; the next collection shown large with a picture of the bin and a text like tomorrow; an address search that finds the round; a night-before reminder time; a list of what goes in which bin with a search for an item and a yes or no answer; a missed bin report with a photo; holiday changes marked on the days.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
