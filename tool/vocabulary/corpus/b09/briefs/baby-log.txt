App: Milk & Moon, a baby feed and sleep log. A quick-add row of big icons for feed, nappy, sleep and medicine; a feed with a timer for the side, an amount in millilitres for a bottle and a stopwatch; a nappy with a wet, dirty or both choice; sleep started with a tap and ended with a tap, a nap clock running; a day as a vertical strip of coloured blocks; a pattern chart over two weeks; a growth entry with a curve against bands; a share to the other parent.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
