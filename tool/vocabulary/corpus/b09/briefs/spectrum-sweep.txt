App: Bandscape, a spectrum analyser. A frequency plot with a live line and a faded peak-hold line behind it, centre and span set by typing or by pinching the plot; a marker placed on a peak with its frequency and level read off; reference level dragged by the top edge; a waterfall strip below painting the past as colours; a table of the five strongest signals; a limit line that turns the trace red when crossed.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
