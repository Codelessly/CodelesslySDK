A long horizontal line of the pipe in kilometre markers with a moving dot for the tool inside; a receiver countdown; a list of findings by position (dent, corrosion, weld) with depth in millimetres and a severity colour; tap a finding to see a cross-section drawing of the wall with the loss shaded; valve states along the line; a report with findings sorted worst first.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
