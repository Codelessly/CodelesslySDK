App: Chronicle, a company timeline. A horizontal timeline from founding to today, with events as cards above and below, dragged sideways and zoomed by decade; filters by product, people and money as colour layers; a card with a photo, a date, a paragraph and a link to related events; a chart of headcount under the line aligned to the years; a hidden period shown as a gap; a play mode stepping through milestones with an animation; a search by year.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
