App: Ledgerly: a personal budgeting app. Screens and features: Home (this month's spending against the budget as a ring with what is left, accounts with balances, the last five transactions, a bar chart of the past six months), Transactions (a list grouped by day with a merchant, a category mark and an amount, income in green; search that forgives; filters for category, account and an amount range set with a slider of two thumbs; a row swiped to change category or delete, the same in its menu, delete asking first; several selected to recategorise), Add transaction (a sheet with two stops on a phone and a dialog at desktop width: an amount field that steps with its arrows and holds while pressed, expense or income, category as chips, account select, date, a note, a repeat switch; save disabled until there is an amount; saving announces it), Budgets (a category list with a slider each, a bar of spent against budget that warns past 90 percent, the total against income), Goals (three savings goals with progress and a projected date that follows a monthly amount slider), Categories (reordered by a handle, renamed, a colour chosen), Settings (currency, first day of the month). Style: trustworthy and light: white ground, deep green for money in, a warm red for over budget, large clear numerals, rounded bars and rings, with special focus on the amount range slider and filters, the add-transaction sheet with its stops, and the budgets recomputing live.
