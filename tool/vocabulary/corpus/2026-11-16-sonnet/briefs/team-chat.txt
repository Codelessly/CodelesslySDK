App: Murmur: a messaging app for small teams. Screens and features: Conversations (a list of channels and direct messages with unread counts and the last message, a search field that forgives typing mistakes and marks what matched, a new-conversation dialog where several people are picked from a list), Thread (messages grouped by sender and by day with a date divider, the newest at the foot; the view stays at the newest message as messages arrive and stops following when the reader scrolls up, with a pill that returns to the newest; a message's menu by right click or long press with react, reply, copy and delete, delete asking first; reactions as chips under a message that count and toggle; a reply quoting the message above it; pictures in a message as a small grid that opens a viewer with zoom; a typing line), Composer (a field that grows with its text up to five lines, an emoji popover, an attach menu, send disabled while it is empty, Enter sends and Shift+Enter breaks the line), Details (members, shared pictures, mute and pin switches; a side pane at desktop width, a sheet with a half and a full stop on a phone), Settings (profile, notification switches, theme). Shortcuts for search, next and previous conversation.

Style: soft and focused: warm white, graphite text, one violet accent for what is yours, bubbles with gentle corners, small quiet timestamps, with special focus on the thread following its newest message, the message menu and reactions, and the details pane becoming a sheet on a phone.

Write every screen of the app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
