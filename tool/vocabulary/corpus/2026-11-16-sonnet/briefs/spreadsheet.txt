App: Gridline: a lightweight spreadsheet. Screens and features: Sheet (a grid of twenty-six columns by fifty rows with lettered column headers and numbered row headers that stay in place while the cells scroll; a cell chosen by tap and moved by the arrows, Tab and Enter; a range chosen by a drag, by Shift with the arrows and by a header for a whole row or column, shown with a frame and a tint; typing replaces a cell, Enter or F2 edits it in place, Escape gives up the edit; a formula bar that shows and edits the chosen cell and names it, as B4; a cell that begins with = is worked out from other cells for sums, averages, minimum, maximum and plain arithmetic, follows them when they change, and shows an error mark when it cannot be; numbers at the right, text at the left), Format (a toolbar: bold, italic, text and fill colour from a popover, align, number format as plain, currency, percent and date, each with a tooltip; applied to the whole range), Rows and columns (a header's menu with insert, delete asking first when there is data, sort ascending and descending; a column's width dragged at its edge and moved by keyboard), Fill (copy, cut and paste of a range with shortcuts; clear), a status line with the count, sum and average of the chosen range, Sheets (tabs at the foot that are added, renamed in place, reordered by drag and removed asking first), Find (a small panel that marks every match and steps through them), Undo and redo, three starting sheets (a budget, a timetable, an inventory). Everything outlives a reload.

Style: plain and exact: white cells with pale grey lines, a green frame for the chosen range, tabular numerals, a quiet toolbar, with special focus on the grid driven wholly by keyboard, range selection with its live status line, and formulas that follow their cells.
