App: Postbox: an email client. Screens and features: Mailbox (three panes: folders with unread counts, a message list, the open message; the dividers between panes drag and move by keyboard and remember where they were; on a phone one pane at a time), Message list (sender, subject, a line of the body, time, an attachment mark, unread in bold; select with click, Shift for a range and Ctrl to add, select all, a bar of bulk actions with the count selected; a row swiped to archive or delete on a phone with the same actions in its menu; filter chips for unread, starred and attachments; empty states for an empty folder and no results), Reading pane (headers that expand, the body, attachment tiles, a quoted thread that collapses, reply, forward, star, move to a folder from a menu, delete asking first), Compose (a dialog with To as chips completed from contacts, subject, body, an attach tile, send disabled until there is a recipient; closing it with text written asks whether to keep the draft), Search (forgiving, matches marked, grouped by folder), Settings (signature, density, shortcuts list). Style: calm and dense: white panes with hairline dividers, slate type, one teal accent, tight rows, generous reading measure in the open message, with special focus on the three panes and their dividers at three widths, range selection with bulk actions, and the compose dialog that guards what was typed.
