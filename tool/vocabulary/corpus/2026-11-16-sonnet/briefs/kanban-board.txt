App: Stack: a project board for a small product team. Screens and features: Board (four columns with a count and a work-in-progress limit that turns the header to a warning when exceeded; cards with a title, labels, an assignee and a due date that is marked when late; a card dragged within a column and to another column, and moved by keyboard; a quick-add field at the foot of each column; a column menu with sort and clear, clear asking first), Card dialog (title and description fields, a checklist with a progress bar where items are added, ticked, reordered and removed, a label picker with several choices, an assignee menu, a due date field, comments that follow the newest, delete asking first; it does not close on an outside press while a field has been changed), List view (the same cards as a table sorted by a header, with rows selected by click, Shift and Ctrl for bulk move and archive), Filters (search, labels, assignee and due as chips with a result count and a no-results state), Archive (restore). The board outlives a reload. Shortcuts: new card, search, switch view, with their hints shown.

Style: crisp and workmanlike: cool grey ground, white cards, one blue accent, coloured label dots, compact type, clear focus rings, with special focus on dragging and keyboard-moving cards between columns, the card dialog with its checklist, and bulk selection in the list view.

Write every screen of the app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls).
