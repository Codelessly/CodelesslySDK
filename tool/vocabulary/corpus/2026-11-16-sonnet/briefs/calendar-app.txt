App: Margin: a calendar for one person's work and home. Screens and features: Month (a calendar in the page with events as coloured bars up to three a day and a count for more, today marked, moved by keyboard, a day opening its events), Week (seven columns of hours with events as blocks placed by time and length, a line at the current time, an all-day row), Day and Agenda (a list by day with empty days skipped and a no-events state), a mini month beside the views that jumps to a day and marks days with events, New and edit event (a dialog: title, calendar as a select with a colour dot, all-day switch, start and end dates as a range picked on a calendar, times, repeat choices, guests as chips, a note; end before start is an error; delete asking first; it keeps what was typed against an outside press), Calendars (a list with a switch each that shows and hides their events), Search (forgiving, results by date). View switch as tabs with shortcuts for today, next, previous, new event and each view; the chosen view and hidden calendars outlive a reload.

Style: light and orderly: white ground, soft grey grid lines, six clear calendar colours, a neutral sans with tabular numerals, with special focus on the month and week views at three widths, the event dialog with its date range, and moving through days by keyboard.
