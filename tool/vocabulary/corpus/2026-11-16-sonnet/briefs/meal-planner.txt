App: Mise: a recipe book and weekly meal planner. Screens and features: Recipes (a grid of twenty with a drawn dish, time, difficulty and a rating; filters for meal, diet and time, a forgiving search over names and ingredients, a no-results state), Recipe (a picture, a servings stepper that rescales every quantity, ingredients as a checklist, steps numbered, nutrition as a small table, a rating set with stars by pointer and keyboard, a note field, save to favourites), Cook mode (one step a page in large type, swiped or moved by arrows, with a progress row; a step with a time has a timer that counts down, can be paused and announces when it ends; the ingredients in a sheet), Week (seven days by three meals as a grid; a recipe dragged from a side list into a slot and moved by keyboard, moved between slots, removed; a slot's menu; the week's totals; on a phone a day at a time), Shopping list (made from the week: ingredients merged and grouped by aisle, ticked, reordered within a group, an item added by a field, clear ticked asking first), Favourites, Add a recipe (a form with ingredients and steps as lists that are added to, reordered and removed, with validation). The week, the list and favourites outlive a reload.

Style: homely and fresh: cream ground, herb green and a tomato accent, a rounded serif for titles, hand-drawn dishes, soft cards, with special focus on the servings stepper rescaling the recipe, dragging recipes into the week, and cook mode with its timers.
