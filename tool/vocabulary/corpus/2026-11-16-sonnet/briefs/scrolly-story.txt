App: Glacier Lines: a scrolling data story about a century of retreating glaciers. Screens and features: Opening (a full-height title over a drawn mountain scene whose layers move at different speeds as the page scrolls, a scroll cue), Chapters (five, each a graphic that stays in place at one side while steps of text scroll past it and change it: a drawn glacier whose ice line retreats step by step; a line chart that draws itself to the year of the step; a map drawing whose regions light in turn; a bar chart whose bars grow; a before and after comparison slider of one valley), a reading-progress line at the top, a chapter rail at the side that marks the current chapter and scrolls to one when pressed, Figures (three large numbers that count up when they come into view), Notes (numbered marks in the text that open a small card on hover, on focus and on a tap, with the source), a table alternative under each chart, Explore (after the story: a region select, a year range slider of two thumbs and a chart that follows them), Method and credits, a share row. On a phone the graphic stays at the top and the steps pass under it. With reduced motion the steps change without animation.

Style: editorial and glacial: paper white and deep slate, ice blues, one warm orange for the present day, a literary serif with a narrow sans for labels, with special focus on the graphic that stays while its steps scroll, the chapter rail following the scroll, and the note cards without hover.
