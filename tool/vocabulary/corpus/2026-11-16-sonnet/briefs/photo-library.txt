App: Contact Sheet: a photo library. Screens and features: Photos (a grid of sixty drawn pictures grouped by month with a heading that stays at the top, three grid sizes from a slider; selected by click, Shift for a range, Ctrl to add and a drag across; a bar with the count selected and add to album, favourite, and delete asking first), Viewer (a picture opened over the grid: zoomed by pinch, wheel and double tap, panned, turned by swipe and arrows, a strip of thumbnails, a count, closed by Escape and a drag down; an info side sheet with date, place, size and tags as chips; favourite; delete asking first), Edit (the picture beside sliders for light, contrast, warmth and a crop ratio choice, changes seen at once, a held button or a divider that compares before and after, reset, save), Albums (a grid of covers, create with a dialog, an album's pictures reordered by drag and keyboard, a picture dragged onto an album, an empty album state), Search (by tag, place and month, forgiving, with chips for the filters), Favourites, Recently deleted (restore, delete for ever asking first). Grid size, albums and favourites outlive a reload.

Style: gallery-like and neutral: near-white ground, black type, no accent but a thin blue for selection, pictures edge to edge with small gaps, with special focus on range selection in the grid, the viewer's zoom and paging, and the edit sliders with the before and after.

Write every screen of the app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
