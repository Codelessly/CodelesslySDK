App: Inkwell: a notes app with notebooks and tags. Screens and features: Workspace (three panes: notebooks as a tree that folds with counts, the notes of the chosen notebook as a list with title, first line, date and tags, the open note; dividers that drag; one pane at a time on a phone), Note (a title field and a long body field that grows, tags as chips added from a field with completion and removed, a pin switch, word count, saved a moment after typing with a saved mark, moved to another notebook from a menu, deleted to the trash asking first), Note list (sort by edited, created or title, pinned first, several selected for move, tag and delete, an empty notebook state), Search (a command palette opened by a shortcut: notes, notebooks and actions in groups, forgiving, matches marked, moved by arrows, Enter opens), Tags (all tags with counts, one tag's notes), Trash (restore, delete for ever asking first, empty the trash), Settings (theme, font size as a slider that changes the note at once, sort). Everything outlives a reload.

Style: paper-like and quiet: cream ground, ink type, a single sepia accent, a bookish serif for the note and a small sans for the chrome, with special focus on the command palette by keyboard, the note saved as it is typed and kept across a reload, and the three panes on a phone.

Write every screen of the app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls).
