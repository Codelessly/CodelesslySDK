App: Zoned, a planning applications viewer. A map of an area with applications as coloured polygons by status (received, approved, refused), a layer for conservation areas and flood zones toggled on a panel; a search by address with a suggestion list; an application page with a timeline of dates, documents list, comments count and a deadline; a comment form with a for or against choice and a text limit; a sign up for alerts within a radius drawn as a circle.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
