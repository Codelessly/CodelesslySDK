App: Epoch, a history explorer. A long horizontal timeline you drag and zoom from millennia down to years, with events as cards clustering into a count when far out and opening when close; two parallel lanes of regions to compare; a card with a picture, a date and a short text; a filter of themes as coloured chips; a map button showing where an event happened; a quiz that asks you to put cards in order.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls).
