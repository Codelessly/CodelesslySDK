App: Tidewatch, a smartwatch face. A round face with an analogue hand clock, a ring around the edge filling with steps, three small dials for battery, next event and temperature; turn the side crown to scroll a stack of cards for the day; raise to wake dimmed to full brightness; a long press opens a gallery of faces swiped sideways with a customise screen to pick colours and which dials show.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
