A clean writing page with a word counter against a target of a hundred showing as a ring that fills and turns gold exactly at the number and red above; a prompt of the day with a countdown; a sentence length bar chart that shows rhythm; a focus mode dimming all but the current sentence; a title field; a draft history; a submit to a monthly contest with a reading preview; a streak of days.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
