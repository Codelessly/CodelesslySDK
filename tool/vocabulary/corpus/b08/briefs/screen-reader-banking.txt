App: Plainsight, a banking app made for a screen reader. Every screen a simple linear order of headings and short items, an account list where each item reads the name, the balance and whether it is low in one sentence; a transfer form with labels, hints and errors announced in the same place; a landmark summary at the top; no motion; a confirm step that restates the amounts in words; a help line button first on every page.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
