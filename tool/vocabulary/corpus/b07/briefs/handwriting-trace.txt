App: Penpal, handwriting practice. A big letter drawn with dotted guide lines and a numbered start dot with arrows; trace with a finger, the ink following only when close to the path and wobbling gently if wrong; a correct stroke order animation showing on request; lines of practice with the baseline and midline; a score of stars per letter; a gallery of finished words; a pen thickness for small hands.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
