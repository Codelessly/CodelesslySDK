App: Soil, a mood journal grown as a garden. Each day adds a plant whose shape and colour come from a mood picked on a wheel of colours; a garden view as a plot of the month you pan across and tap a plant to read its entry; a gratitude line of three short fields; weather and sleep tags; a season of the year graph of moods; an insight card naming what lifts you; a lock; reminders at set times.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
