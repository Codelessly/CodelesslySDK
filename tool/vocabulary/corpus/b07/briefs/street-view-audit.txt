App: Kerbcheck, a street accessibility audit. A strip of street photos you step through with arrows, each placed on a mini map; mark a kerb, a ramp, a crossing or a blocked path by tapping the picture and choosing a type with a severity of three levels; a measurement field with a photo of a tape; a street score by colour along the line on the map; a review queue where a second person confirms; a export for the council.

Write two to four screens of this app, each under a line `### <screen name>`, starting with App (an app window) or Page (a page that scrolls). Overlays (dialogs, sheets, menus) go after the screen that opens them. Realistic content. Every interaction in the brief is written, not described. Output only the notation.
