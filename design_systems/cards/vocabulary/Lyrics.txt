// The words of a song, line by line: lines is a list of {"t": seconds, "text": …} (or plain texts); the line sounding at time (or bind) is large and in the highlight; a tap on a line seeks to it (onSeek: result is its time).
Lyrics lines=[] time bind=pos h=200 highlight=accent onSeek
  state pos=0
  Row w=fill h={h} stretch
    Column grow gap=8 pad=[0,4,0,4] scroll
      each row in map(lines, l => {"text": coalesce(l.text, l), "t": coalesce(l.t, 0), "on": coalesce(l.t, 0) <= coalesce(time, pos) && count(lines, m => coalesce(m.t, 0) > coalesce(l.t, 0) && coalesce(m.t, 0) <= coalesce(time, pos)) == 0})
        Text "{row.text}" size={row.on ? 20 : 16} weight={row.on ? 700 : 400} color={row.on ? highlight : "inkFaint"} onTap={pos=row.t; onSeek(row.t)}
