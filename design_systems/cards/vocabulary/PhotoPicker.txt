// Where a photo is added: a framed area with a camera icon and its label; a tap runs onPick; once bind holds a photo it says so.
// It opens no camera or file picker: there is no capture primitive yet (the vocabulary loop's D18); the page's onPick sets the photo.
PhotoPicker "label" bind=photo onPick
  state photo=""
  Column w=fill pad=16 gap=4 radius=8 border=#FFCBD5E1 fill=#FFF9FAFB center middle onTap={onPick}
    Icon "photo_camera" size=24 color=#FF6B7280
    Text.muted "{coalesce(label, "Add a photo")}"
    if photo != "" && photo != null
      Text.caption "Photo attached"
