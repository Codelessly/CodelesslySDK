Three ways to extend a layout
From most to least common. Registering a new node type from outside the SDK is not supported.
1 · Inside
Your Flutter widgets
ExternalComponents: maps, ads, camera previews, charts, platform views.
2 · Behind
Your code
Functions, native scripts, and your own services (AnalyticsService, LinkOpener, AuthService...).
3 · Around
Wrapping
layoutBuilder: a SafeArea, an overlay, a RefreshIndicator, a banner.
