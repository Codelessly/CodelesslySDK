What a layout's look comes from
Almost everything is designed. A Theme matters only for what the design leaves to Material.
Designed, on each node
Colors, text styles, radii, shadows. They render the same wherever the layout is embedded.
Aa
From a Theme
Material widgets that keep default colors and shapes; the runtime's toasts, confirm dialogs and pickers.
Default: the project's theme tokens (primary, surface, radius...).
With useHostTheme: true: your app's Theme.
Dark mode
The SDK does not restyle a layout. A layout can adapt with ${screen.isDark} in conditions, bindings and variants.
Fonts
When a layout loads, the SDK fetches and registers its fonts, then shows it.
Fonts come from the project's uploads or Google Fonts, once per run, not cached on disk. For offline apps ship them: NoFontFetcher if they are declared in your pubspec, or a FontFetcher of your own.
